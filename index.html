Our story

It started in our living room.

In July 2026, we watched the World Cup final together at home in California.

A little more than three years earlier, we had left China to start over: a mother and her son, learning a new country, a new language of daily life, building everything from the ground up.

When the final whistle blew and the captain raised the trophy, millions of people were watching a single golden object carry the weight of a lifetime of work. And at that moment, we had the same thought: what if an honor like this were made the way beautiful things are made where we come from? By hand, in cloisonné enamel, fine porcelain or cast bronze, crafts passed from master to apprentice for centuries.

We know something about the long road behind a breakthrough. Anyone who has started over does: the years no one sees, the small victories, the day it finally comes together. That day deserves more than a catalog plaque.

So we founded HyOvation, to honor the people who build extraordinary things with pieces as rare as their achievement. Each one is designed from scratch, made by an artisan's hands, and delivered by us, in person.

Working with us

Good to know before we start

What is the minimum project size?

Projects start at $5,000. We take on a small number of commissions each season so every piece receives full attention.

How does the design fee work?

Concept design costs $1,000 to $3,000 depending on complexity. If you go ahead with the order, the full design fee is credited toward it.

How are payments scheduled?

A 50% deposit confirms your order and starts tooling and production. The balance is due before delivery.

How far ahead should we plan?

Most pieces take 4 to 8 weeks from approved design to delivery. Bronze and sterling silver commissions can take longer, so the earlier we hear the ceremony date, the better.

How are the awards presented?

Each piece arrives in a fitted walnut presentation case with a certificate of authenticity. Limited editions are individually numbered, and for Bay Area ceremonies we can deliver and set up in person.

Can you keep our project confidential?

Yes. Unannounced deals, patents and honorees stay private, and we sign a non-disclosure agreement on request.
