What we collect

When you send a consultation request, we collect the details you provide: your name, email, phone number, organization, event date, budget range and message. Our hosting provider may also record standard technical data such as IP address and browser type.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your request, prepare proposals and deliver your order. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business, such as our website host and email provider, and with craftspeople working on your order when they need it. Everyone we work with is bound by confidentiality.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and project records for as long as needed to serve you and meet legal and tax obligations.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act where it applies. To make a request, email hello@hyovation.com.
